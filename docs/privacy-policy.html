Privacy Policy

Simon Memory Game — Last updated: 2026-05-16

Overview

Simon Memory Game is a free mobile game. We do not collect any personal information ourselves. This policy explains what data third-party services (specifically Google AdMob) collect through our app.

Data We Collect

We (Omer Blecher) do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Google AdMob

Our app displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Android Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address (used to infer approximate location for ad targeting)
  • App interaction data (how you interact with the app and ads)
  • Diagnostic data (crash logs, performance data)

This data is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

For more information, see:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Partner Policies

Your Choices

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via Android device settings: Android Settings > Privacy > Ads > Reset Advertising ID (or "Delete advertising ID" on Android 12+).

EEA and UK users: a consent dialog appears on first launch. You may choose not to consent to personalized ads.

GDPR (EEA and UK Users)

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent dialog before any ads are displayed. This dialog is powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your consent at any time by reinstalling the app.
