       - Purpose: Advertising, Analytics
       - Is data processed ephemerally: No

       - Purpose: Analytics
       - Is data processed ephemerally: No

    4. Device or other IDs → Device or other IDs

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Upload key (.jks) → Play Console | Upload key authenticates the AAB upload; Google re-signs with app signing key before distribution |
| Play Console → public Play Store | Once published, app is available to all Android users; cannot retract quickly |
